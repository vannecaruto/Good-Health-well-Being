Mental Health

Support for mental health

1. Immediate & Crisis Support

WE provide free anonymous phone calls for people in emotional distress.24/7 Phone Calls: With real-time Phone calls with trained volunteers or clinicians for users who prefer to be anonymous.

2. Self-Help Tools & Service Navigation

Public health portals and educational sites help users understand symptoms and find local care.Interactive Wellbeing Tools, Mood and thought diaries, stress check-ins, personalised action plans, and guided breathing or mindfulness exercises We connect you with a licensed professional suited to your needs.

3. Peer & Community Support.

Community-led support groups reduce isolation by connecting people with others who lives with the same experience. Support groups tailored to specific challenges like depression, anxiety, eating disorders, grief, or addiction.
